## The chronicle's tenets

Agreed at the table in Session Zero.

- Avoid Isolation (vampires need to be near humanity, lest they lose theirs)
- No engaging in torture of humans, or witnessing without intervening. (this is left for the Sabbat, but is seen as needlessly cruel and a risk to humanity)
- No killing someone by feeding (not only seen as needless, but wasteful and a risk to the Masquerade)

## Lines & Veils

- No rape, torture of women.
- No detailed descriptions of children on fire or similar.
