---
title: Debriefing
part: Monday, 19 March 2012
---

There was a pile of clothes. Tony found slacks, loafers and a collared shirt. Alice found
something that did not draw the eye.

Lady Catherine went to one of the prisoners instead, a young professional woman, and let the
woman see her properly. "Oh my goodness," she said, "I would love to borrow that outfit for a day
or two. Don't you just look amazing." The woman was star-struck. She stripped where she stood and
handed it over, and Catherine gave her something from the pile in exchange.

---

Dressed, they were taken upstairs. The building was a warehouse, pallets stacked on the floor
below, but the office at the top was another thing: polished wood, leather, books. Darius Shirazi
sat behind the desk. A heavy-set man sat by the wall with the look of a fixer or a bodyguard.
That was Mickey.

Darius made the introductions and asked how he could be of help. Da Silva excused himself and
took the chalice with him.

Alice had Darius's measure quickly. Eager, and very careful with it.

Some things they already knew. Roger de Camden had been close to Mithras and had helped with the
ritual. Queen Anne had been close to him too, and had held London more than once while he was
away. Darius filled in the rest without being pressed. Anne had been Prince of London since
Mithras disappeared. The accepted story was that he had died when the Germans bombed the city.
The cult knew better. There had been a traitor, and an attempt on his life, and the cult believed
he had lived through it and was alive now, in hiding.

Tony went around the obvious question. Alice asked it straight.

"Why weren't we woken as soon as Mithras went missing?"

"Because the same bombs brought the buildings down on top of you," Darius said. "You were buried
for decades. You were only just found, and that by accident."

---

He handed Tony a sealed scroll. Tony was still turning it over to look at the seal when Alice
took it out of his hands.

The seal meant something to Catherine. She remembered being jealous, and badly. She read over
Alice's shoulder. Darius plainly wanted to know what it said and did not ask. Tony glanced at it
and left the reading to them.

Catherine read it aloud, all but the part about what was to be done.

Decorum, Alice said, required that they present themselves to the Queen and make their
introductions again. There was an Elysium on Friday night. The Queen was giving a party.

They noticed that the letter said *Lady* Anne. Darius called her Queen.

---

Alice and Catherine saw it at the same moment: movement outside the window, and a few small beads
of red light, hanging in the dark.

Catherine pointed. Darius spun round in his chair. Mickey was already up and moving for the door,
and Darius had a briefcase open and was sweeping papers into it.

"We can't leave without the chalice," Catherine whispered.

Alice went out into the hall after Mickey. Tony waited outside the office. Catherine stayed to help
Darius pack, and to tell him that they had to go now. Da Silva came back, and Tony told him they
needed the chalice, and he handed it over.

Darius was still looking at the room. Catherine made it an order.

"We need to leave, *now*!"

He gave the office one despairing look and snapped the briefcase shut. "We'll go through the
basement. There's a hidden way out."

Tony went with him. Da Silva went the other way, to help Mickey.

---

From the mezzanine Alice watched Mickey change. He got bigger. He jumped the rail and landed
running. There were two officers below in SWAT gear. He went over the top of the first one, and
the second emptied a magazine into him.

Alice ran back for the others. The office was empty. She went back the way she had come and
caught them as they were starting down a new stairwell.

"Darius, what the fuck's going on?"

"Mickey and Adelino will buy us time," Darius said, "against whoever that was."

He took them through a large drainage pipe and out onto a ledge above the Thames, and along it.
Catherine slipped and went into the river.

Behind them a boat came in against the warehouse they had just left, and more
figures climbed out of it. Catherine dragged herself up the bank without her jacket. Darius
unlocked a white van, went back for her, and drove them all to another safe house.

They agreed there that nothing unnecessary would be shared with anyone who was not a herald.

They read the scroll again. The place to start, they decided, was the chantry Alice had belonged
to, and Sri Sansa, if he could still be found.
