---
title: Cleaning Up
part: Saturday, 24 March 2012
---

Catherine looked around. "Where's my puppet? Did you see her?"

"Briefly, on my way down," said Matthias. "I got hold of a mop and tried to walk past them."

Catherine left Gabrielle to Matthias and whatever he meant to do, and went to the one human still alive,
and questioned him.

Her eyes had gone red and yellow, an animal's. "How did you know we were down here?"

A little way off, Adisa had his rifle and his pistol, a couple of knives and several wooden stakes, and was
pouring what looked like holy water out onto the ground. "These people came *prepared*."

"How the *fuck* did you know about us?"

"We got a call from the officers watching the street. They had an informant."

"I *see*. That *whore*. Did you see what the informant looked like?"

"I assume it was the woman standing with them when we got here."

"Matthias? Feel like coming up to the street with me? I feel like ripping heads." She took the pistol from
Adisa and gave her the knife. The Masquerade was already broken. Catherine felt no qualms at all about killing
the humans.

---

Matthias was in a bad way, but he went with Adisa and Catherine. When they got back to the station, Victor
asked if they were leaving, and whether he could come. He agreed to protect them from the rear.

They tried to come up quietly and did not manage it. The woman officer heard something. "Sounds like they're
coming back up. Hopefully they got those terrorists." A pause. Then Claire's voice: "That's not them. We need
to run."

Catherine went up the stairs at a run. Adisa went faster and met the male officer at the corner as he came
round it. He took a knife in the gut, and Adisa's claws closed on his throat.

Catherine reached the top just after, tried to shoot past Adisa and wing the woman officer, and missed. It
made her furious.

"Claire! Get your arse over here, *now*." She turned to Matthias. "Restrain these fucks."

Matthias did not even hear her. He called up the shadow arms and choked the man Adisa was holding until he
went limp.

Catherine went for the woman officer and tried to club her with the butt of the pistol. The officer saw it
coming a mile off, turned it into a glancing blow with her baton, and swung back. The baton cracked into
Catherine's jaw and knocked it out of joint. Her body took it, and nothing else broke.

Claire's eyes had gone glassy. She had turned round and was walking back.

Matthias sent the shadow arms across. One wrapped the woman officer's right leg and pulled her knee down to
the pavement. She fought to stay up.

Catherine ignored the pain and put her jaw back. She caught the officer's eye. "Kneel."

The woman knelt, and bowed, and kept her chin up to hold Catherine's gaze.

"I think we're clear," Adisa called. Victor ran up, and ran off.

---

Catherine hit Claire across the face and knocked her sideways. Claire whimpered. Catherine hit her again, and
again. Claire tried to get an arm up and could not. Adisa turned her back. Matthias left Catherine to her human.

"I gave you a gift. I gave you the chance to serve me, and maybe to become one of us, and you *betray* me? How
long? Is my banker part of this? *Answer me*."

Claire's eyes were full of terror. "I had no idea. I had no idea they were going to do that and I didn't know
what to do. I'm sorry."

"The penalty for your betrayal is death." Catherine asked Adisa for the knife. Adisa gave it to her, against
her better judgement. Claire had burst into tears. She was gasping, and she had nothing left.

"She risked the Masquerade," said Catherine, and she stabbed Claire to death. Claire cried out once, sharply,
and then the moaning stopped.

Later Catherine would see it for what it was. She had believed Claire didn't know. She had been driven by
something in her blood. None of that would undo it.

---

Matthias found the police dog and killed it. He killed the woman officer, and took his time turning the
officers' bodies to ash. He carried the dog's body down into the station and brought it back up out of death,
and named it Carlo.

Catherine carried Claire down to the station and found a stretch of tunnel where she could heap a grave over
her. First she searched her. She took her purse: a flip phone with some numbers in it, her ID card, some cash
and cards.

---

Matthias set Carlo on a scent, and they followed him back to where the ambush had been. The man they had been
questioning was gone. So were Alice and Tony.

Matthias was getting hungrier.

They went on behind Carlo, and came to a train stopped dead on the tracks. They followed it along, past a
tunnel branching off to the right, until Carlo brought them to a carriage with its windows smashed and some of
them blocked. Catherine went to the door. It hung off its hinges, and inside was a mess. This, she understood,
was the nest of the thing that had broken up the fight. She told the others. They decided to leave it alone and
try the tunnel they had passed.

---

At last they came near another derelict platform, and Carlo pricked up. They looked twice. There was a woman
sitting on the edge of the platform.

![A woman in a red jumper sitting on a dark Underground platform](campaign/assets/gm/fall-of-london-the-story-so-far-untitled-2.webp)

"Is this the way to Richard de Worde?" Catherine called.

The woman went from carefree to on her feet and backing away in an instant. "Please, please don't hurt me!"

Matthias looked at her with the sight. Kindred. Clanless, but no thin-blood. She had been trying, or was still
trying, to make herself unseen. And she was nowhere near as scared as she was acting.

"We intend you no harm," said Catherine.

Matthias sent Carlo on ahead. "I think this may be a trap."

"Look, sister," said Catherine, "we mean you no harm. We're just trying to find this Nosferatu. He's supposed to
have something for us. I'm going to reach into this bag, and it's not a weapon." She took out a necklace. "It's
something for you, if you'll show us where to find him."

The woman looked at it warily. "He doesn't like strangers. He's really shy. But if he likes you, he's the best
guy in the world. He told me to keep an eye on you."

"All right. But we need to see him. He has something we need. I have some information I'll trade him for it. He
likes information, doesn't he?"

"He loves it. And I like the jewellery. Have you got more?"

"I'll have more for you once we've found him."

"I guess... I guess I can take you to a place, and have him meet you there. Like an entryway. Then I'll go and
find him."

Matthias read her, and read her clearly. Nearly all of it was true, except the part about taking them to Richard
de Worde. She had no intention of doing that.

"Yes," said Matthias, "you can fetch Richard for us. Our mistress, Queen Anne, will be very pleased." He was
trying to tell the others something without saying it.

"OK, well, follow me." She set off down the tunnel as if she owned it.

---

They followed the tunnels a while, then went through a door into maintenance passages, and out into corridors of
grating and huge pipes, then more passages, a staircase down, and another door. She opened it on a big
maintenance room. Piled pipes blocked the far door. There were lockers along the walls, and marks in chalk and
blood where some kind of ritual had been done.

Matthias, Adisa and Catherine were all hungry now.

"Well, this is where de Worde will meet you. I'll tell him Queen Anne's emissaries are here to see him. Can I see
the necklace?"

"No!" said Matthias.

"I'd feel better if you went and got him first," said Catherine.

"You said the *other* pieces were for when I got him. Half now, half later."

"What assurance do I have?"

"That's why you're only giving me half."

"Fine." Catherine broke the necklace in two. She had noticed that there was only one clear way out of the room,
the way they had come in.

"You give me both halves, like we agreed. I get you de Worde, like we agreed. Then you give me the other jewellery,
like we agreed."

Catherine stepped forward as if to hand it over, and brushed a finger down the woman's cheek.

She was hungry, and she was irritated, and this slip of a girl was trying to stand between her and *de Worde*.
Richard had been Mithras' confidant for *centuries* before this girl existed. The whole weight of Catherine came
down on her, and the girl simply folded.

"Now there," said Catherine. "Wouldn't you like to take us to de Worde?"

"You know, I'd do anything to see you happy. I can't wait for him to meet you. I'm sure he'll love you like he
loves me. He's been so good to me, and I think you'll really appreciate him."

Her name, they learned, was Rea. She led them on for another half-hour or so, until they came to something like
a bunker with a wheel on its vault door. Rea opened it and took them into the quarters of Richard de Worde.
