There had been a fifth coffin under the rubble. The Puttanescas had their own ways of knowing
things, and they had it out of the ground before even Darius could get to it. Matthias Darkwood was
woken by one of Mithras' own and walked through the modern world until he could stand up in it.

Within a day a letter from Pater Thomas found him too. He excused himself and went looking for the
other heralds and for the relics of Mithras. His first lead was in Southall.

---

Catherine excused herself and promised to catch up.

Alice and Tony went west looking for Sri Sansa. Tony hailed a cab. The driver was an easygoing
Egyptian who enjoyed Tony's company so much that he forgot to charge them when he let them out in
the middle of Southall.

It was not yet midnight and the street was full of teenagers. Cars went by with bhangra coming out
of every window. A knot of boys was squaring up for a fight, and an older boy was egging it on.
After the petrol station Tony wanted no part of it. They went into the Roti Mojo.

There was a queue for tables. Tony tried charm on the hostess. She made a point of showing him the
ring on her finger. When he said they were there to see Najma, though, she nodded and took them
through a curtain at the back, into a room of low couches.

An East African woman was finishing some business with an older man. He bowed and left. The
hostess told the woman her guests had arrived, and Tony and Alice sat down without being asked.

Alice looked at her with the sight the blood gives, and the Beast came up into her throat. Najma
was another duskborn, another abomination like the fortune teller. Alice was appalled at how far
things had fallen. She swallowed the disgust. She did not offer her hand.

"We were told you might know where to find someone."

Najma had already worked out that Alice was Tremere. "Are you looking for Bainbridge, the primogen?"

Alice remembered Edward Bainbridge. He had been Primogen in her day, too. "No. Sri Sansa."

"Why do you seek him?"

"Not your concern. Are you his gatekeeper?"

"I am not. But I know where his side entrances are."

"Tell us, and I can ensure that you are adequately compensated."

"A boon?"

Alice hesitated. "A trivial boon, perhaps." As far as she was concerned this was not a negotiation
between equals.

"I will reward you appropriately," Tony said.

Najma was not impressed by Alice. "How about a vial of vitae? From either of you?"

Alice laughed at her.

"Then I think we are done here."

"I can give you a vial of vitae," Tony said.

"What the fuck, Tony?"

"How else are you planning on finding Sansa?"

Alice said very little and kept her hands to herself. Najma took out some medical kit and drew a
vial of blood from Tony's arm, and thanked him.

Alice snatched the vial out of her hand and held it away. Najma bared her teeth. They were
ridiculously small.

Alice gave the vial to Tony. "Keep this. Do *not* give it to her." To Najma she said, "You will pass
Sri Sansa a message to find me. There is no reward unless contact is made. If this happens, we
will *consider* your request."

"I know where to find him tomorrow," Najma said. "I can give you that now. Not without the vial."

"Not happening."

Najma took a sip from a flask in her handbag. A mist came up around Tony and he could not see, and
he began to choke. Something took the vial out of his hand. Alice spat venomous blood at the shape
going for the door. When the mist cleared Najma was gone. On the seat where she had been sitting
there was a piece of paper.

Matthias came by train and got thoroughly lost. He picked up one of the free local papers. He passed
the same gang of boys shouting at each other, and went well round them, and bought a map at a
corner shop.

The map brought something back. He had been to an Elysium in Southall in the 1930s. He found the
place: the Blue Monsoon Lounge, which did not look as though anyone had touched it in decades, and
which was moderately busy. His sight told him Kindred had used it, but not much. It was too run
down to have held an Elysium in years.

There was a man at the bar who was very drunk. Matthias got him into the toilets, wedged the door
with a bin, and put a hand over his mouth while he fed. Someone called through the door. Matthias
said his friend was very sick and he was looking after him. He drank the man dry, and then he
turned him to ash.

Coming out, he nearly walked into a woman in a hijab. She looked him over.

"Was it a good feed?"

---

Catherine had stayed behind at the parade after all, and had her own fortune read. The Star: hope,
a turning point, good prospects.

The fortune teller knew one name she asked about. Angelique Darkwood had been a member of the
Mithraic cult. He had never met her, and he did not know where she was now.
