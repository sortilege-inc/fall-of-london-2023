---
title: Out of the Frying Pan
part: Saturday, 24 March 2012
---

Matthias's body had been going about the world without him. Then his mind clicked back into place, and he was
in Calogero's limousine, and Calogero was watching him.

"Ah. You're back."

"Don't know where I went."

"I had word from the Nosferatu that I was to collect you from outside an Underground station. What were you
doing?"

"Well, I was looking for a Nosferatu, and then I woke up here."

"It seems your company wasn't wanted."

"It generally isn't."

"Well, in any case I'll have to thank him for the chance to congratulate you in person. I hear you had
something to do with our late Seneschal's passing."

Matthias was not sure they had in fact killed Valerius. He nodded. "I had a small part to play."

"Between that and whatever happened at the Elysium, which I was lucky enough to have left early, it seems
there are openings to be had. Not only primogen of the Hecata. Perhaps prince. I've been in touch with the
Justicar, Juliette Parr, about the possibility. Queen Anne may not be the leader we need in times like these."

"I'm shocked, *shocked*. Of *course* she isn't the leader we need. Any goat can see that."

"Seeing it, and bringing it about, are different things."

"I've been awake a week and her rule is already falling apart."

"In the past few months I've come into something I believe will give us leverage." He nodded at the empty
seat to his left, and when Matthias looked at it with the sight, it came into focus: the ghost of a haughty
woman, whispering in Calogero's ear. "A Ventrue owed me a great boon once, and met her final death before she
could pay it. I have been making use of her services, learning some of the Ventrue's less savoury secrets.

"You should look in on Lady Scarlett and reassure her about whatever it is you seem to have done. I'm sure
she's delighted with the recent vacancy. Only a fool could miss that she was being groomed for higher office.
And I'm sure the Keeper of Elysium, Regina Blake, is very relieved that it will be Scarlett in line for
Seneschal and not herself. Both of them are worth keeping sweet until the Justicar can weigh in."

"Smiley and polite until it's time not to be."

---

The car drew up, and Matthias saw Alice storm into the building with Lady Catherine some way behind her.

"You missed a complete shitshow," said Catherine.

"Sorry about that. No idea what happened. Woke up in a limo. Don't even remember going to sleep."

"Well, that arsehole said his debt to your family is paid."

"Didn't know he owed. Glad he's keeping up with his bills. Did you get anything useful?"

"He's a total dick and he can't be trusted. He has the dagger, and he's using it for some kind of blood
ritual. Remember that feeling in the tunnels?"

"Got pretty hungry pretty quick."

"That was him. We were his victims, not his guests. My instinct for staying alive kicked in and I said what
I thought he wanted to hear to get us out, and now Alice thinks I'm an apostate."

"Apostate? Not catching, I hope."

"She'll come round. She takes our lord's service a bit too seriously."

"Enthusiastic."

"Fucking fanatic."

"Do we know how to deal with his sorcery?"

"Well, de Worde doesn't want to answer the Beckoning. He isn't going to give up the dagger."

They caught each other up: Alice's working, Valerius's death. Matthias began to plan. He would learn the
Underground, and find some way to counter the sorcery.

---

Inside, Tony was already home, and in a shouting match with Alice about Ferhat.

Catherine went round them and found Oliver. It was done, he told her, and the evidence cleaned up as well as
it could be. She took out a ring, put it on his finger, and drew him into the bedroom.

Matthias got himself some bagged blood and took over the study. After some reading he sent word to his
retainer to get the plans for a section of the Underground.

Alice stormed in, and Matthias talked her into helping him work out how to hold off the hunger magic down
there. She did some research of her own and found reason to think that certain artefacts could do it.

She also sent word to Oskar Anasov, asking how to reach Gwenllian ferch Arwyn.

---

Catherine rang Lady Scarlett and got a recorded message. *This number cannot receive calls.* She rang Darius.

"Who is this?"

"Lady Catherine."

"Oh, thank goodness."

"I'm trying to get a message to Queen Anne. The task she gave us is done."

"I don't have a direct line to her. She's... did you not hear?"

"I heard about the attack on the Elysium."

"Yes. A number of Kindred are dead. The Sheriff of North London is gone, and others. I'd guess going through
Valerius, or Regina Blake, or Lady Scarlett would make the most sense."

"Valerius went up in a bomb."

"The humans got him too?"

"Yes."

"This is far worse than I thought. Well, Regina has a club, The Trunk, and I can't see her ever leaving it. Or
Lady Scarlett..."

"I tried her. She isn't answering."

"I have a dead drop. I'll pass your message on." He rang off.

---

The next night Matthias's retainer had left a fat envelope. The thin paper unfolded into big sheets: the
tunnels, section by section, over an area about a mile and a half across. Above the part where they had been
was mostly shops and light industry. A public park sat over another section. There were far too many ways in
to watch them all. The big passages were clear on the plans. The doors and ladders were not.

Catherine and Matthias talked about blocking the entrances, laying siege, smoking him out. There were too many
entrances; none of it would work. They did find several other ways back to the bunker that might serve.

"What if the dagger isn't hidden?" said Catherine. "What if he has it on him? How the hell do we bring him
down? We still have some of the toys you took from Anne."

"I'm not sure my shotgun and my vest will count for much. He put the whammy on me before I'd even got through
the door, and he looked more than a match for the three of you."

"Four. Three of us came back. Is there some way we could spoil his ritual? Make it backfire?"

Alice came in. She had seen Laila, and they had made necklaces for all of them. She believed, cautiously, that
they might hold off the effect.

"I'm going to The Trunk," said Catherine. "Maybe Regina, or someone, knows something that gives us an edge on
de Worde."

---

They took a cab. Nobody asked for ID; they just got a stamp on the hand. The dance floor was packed and there
was a drag show on the stage. On one of the balconies stood an ornate couch, and on it sat Regina, holding
court, with a young man's head in her lap that she was stroking and a woman kneeling before her whose chin she
held in her hand as she spoke.

"Welcome, and drink. My domain is your domain tonight. Come and talk once you've had your fill."

Alice found a young man and brought him over. She asked what brought him there. He liked the vibe, he said. His
housemate was one of the performers. He didn't always remember his nights here. He pointed people out, and
pointed out Regina: she *wasn't* the owner, but she was the queen of the place, always there, always with
people hanging on her every word. He'd love to get close to her. His name was Malik.

Alice asked about rumours. There had been some disappearances, he said. Some people thought it was trafficking.
But the management had been on it, said they'd put safeguards in place, and there hadn't been any trouble for a
year or so.

Matthias sat at the bar and paid them no attention. He was talking to Carlo, the dead dog, whom he had left in
an alley outside. *Find a rat and kill it for me.*

Catherine had found four or five people to fawn over her in a booth tucked out of the way.

Malik was describing blissed-out experiences, some allowed and some not, and Alice asked what he meant. He took
her upstairs, past Catherine, to some low diwaniya couches. A woman was cradling a man's body,
kissing him or perhaps feeding on him. Malik went past her to a couple holding hands on a sofa, and introduced
them as Tibor and Lila. He'd been with them before, he said, and their kisses were electric. Alice saw at once
that they were Kindred. Gangrel, probably.

Carlo reported to Matthias: one rat, two, many, many rats. Matthias went out and collected the bodies to begin
raising an army of rats to watch for him.

---

Catherine got herself free at last and presented herself to Regina. "I've been trying to get in touch with the
Prince. I heard there was bad news."

Regina waved her hangers-on back and got up. "We can talk in my office."

She took Catherine through a curtain, down a corridor, through another curtain and a door, into a modest,
dimly lit office. Filing cabinets, a desk, and a giant mural of a vagina. She sat and waved Catherine to the
chair opposite.

"So you haven't heard from her either."

"I have not."

"I've never had an Elysium go wrong like that. I wouldn't be surprised if I were replaced at court. I have no
idea who those people were. It's amazing I got out."

"They were human?"

"Yes. But they fought with fire. They knew what they were doing."

Catherine told her about the team they had met in the tunnels, and some of the rest, without saying where it
had come from.

"I think she's gone to ground," said Regina, "and is waiting till things are safe before she says anything.
But I haven't had a word. Somebody picked up my retainer a couple of nights ago, drunk and disorderly and
possession, and he's who I'd normally use as a go-between in a situation like this. Trenton. They're holding
him at Bishopsgate. If you could get him out, I'd owe you a boon."

"I'm not sure I can do that. I only wanted to tell Queen Anne that the task she gave me is done."

"It's near certain I'll hear from her in the next day or two. I can pass it on. I can't promise I'll still be
Keeper afterwards."

"It isn't your fault, Gina. You didn't invite them in."

"Let's be clear. If you were prince you'd do the same. Anyway. If you can even find out how Trenton is doing,
I'll owe you a minor boon. A major one if he comes back to me safe."

"I'll find him. On another matter: do you know how I could reach the Justicar?"

"Her I *can* reach. How should she contact you?"

Catherine gave her number.

"Great. I'll be in touch."
