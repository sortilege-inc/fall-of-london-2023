---
title: Queen Anne's Elysium
part: Friday, 23 March 2012
---

When Alice got back to the safe house Darius was clearing it out, and he sent her on to the new
haven. The address was a flat, and a lovely one. Catherine's finance man let her in and opened the
wall to the suite behind it.

The others were already settled. Alice went straight to sleep.

When she woke she found there was a *thin-blood* in the place too.

She showed them the ring. She was wearing it on a chain around her neck.

---

Catherine rang the number Darius had given her for the party. Lady Scarlett Churchill answered.
They had been in business together in the thirties. They talked about nothing much, and in the
middle of it Lady Scarlett mentioned that she had given up both Catherine and Angelique for dead
in the Blitz.

Catherine remembered digging through rubble with her hands, and digging, until she found her
love's broken body.

Lady Scarlett gave her the address and the arrangements, and told her to bring a gift. Then she
apologised and rang off.

For the gift, Catherine drew blood from both her ghouls into small crystal vials.

---

The party was at the Shard. Alice went to the guards on the door they had been told to use and
showed them her fangs.

The Elysium took up the sixty-sixth and sixty-seventh floors. Lady Scarlett was the majordomo, and
she took Catherine in her arms as she stepped out of the lift.

"It's so good to see you. You're looking well."

Catherine gave her the vials. "A gift for her majesty."

"I'll make sure she gets them. It's probably been a while. Enjoy the Elysium. There are vessels for
your enjoyment, to every taste. Do you seek an audience?"

"If she has time for me, I'd be honoured."

"Then we'll get you a slot."

---

Matthias looked around. Salvatrice Giovanni was almost certainly somewhere in the room, but the one
he wanted was his own contact, Calogero Puttanesca, who was just leaving a conversation.

"Thank you for delivering my mail. How did you get hold of that letter so quickly?"

"The cult of Mithras, weak and scattered as it is, still has its ears," said Calogero, "and Pater
Thomas has ways of knowing things that I try to stay clear of. Please don't take offence, but Queen
Anne declared him dead decades ago, when she took power. It's a lot easier for us now, honestly. I
was surprised Pater Thomas was still active."

"How has our family fared?"

"Our family has fared well. The hardest thing has been the court. It's calcified. The primogens are
weak and insipid, and I think Queen Anne likes them that way; they're easier to control. But our
clan's interests would be far better served by a strong figurehead."

"Do you have someone in mind?"

"That's always the question, isn't it? Before you came back, it was Salvatrice or me. But there are
more Hecata in London now, and we need some leadership that does more than fawn at the Ventrue's
feet."

"I've preferred to lead from the background. The figurehead is, well, just a figurehead."

"I hear the group you arrived with has an audience with Queen Anne later. You might put in a good
word for me. See if she'd be open to changing things a little, even for a while."

"I'll see what I can do."

"Is there anything I can do for you?"

Matthias asked about Richard de Worde. Calogero talked about the Nosferatu in general, and how much
they relied on modern technology. He was old-fashioned himself. He liked his informants flesh and
blood, and he liked nothing written down.

---

A man who could have been a model offered Alice his wrist, and she took it. As she drank she
listened to an argument between a Nosferatu called Caleb and a Ventrue called Natasha over hunting
grounds they both claimed.

She heard both of them out, and took Caleb aside to ask about Richard de Worde. He said he could
tell her where to find him.

Alice could see that both of them were reaching. Natasha, perhaps, thought the other Ventrue at court
would back her, and was throwing her weight about. Alice found for Caleb. Natasha took it with bad
grace. Caleb told Alice that de Worde could be found at a certain Underground station.

---

Catherine drifted over to Bainbridge. "Did you find anything?"

"What I have is a *lack* of information, which I hope will help you."

"How is that helpful?"

"I have witnesses to her death. None to her *final* death."

"And the location?"

He gave her a slip of paper. "It's all flats now. Ironmark Holdings."

She kissed his cheek. "Thank you."

---

In pieces, from several people, Alice heard the same rumour. Gwenllian ferch Arwyn was not here.
Her childe had been killed, supposedly by mortal hunters. Chun Hei had sworn revenge and was in the
room somewhere.

Catherine, Alice and Matthias went looking for Chun Hei. On the way they passed a Kindred sitting
with her arms around her knees, rocking. She was *hungry*, and she was in a bad way.

Catherine went and took two of the vessels by the wrists and walked them over to her. The Kindred
barely spoke. She took the man's wrist and drank deep, and moved to the woman in good time. Bringing
two had been wise.

"Thank you. I didn't think I'd be able to leave this party without tearing something apart."

"Why did you come here so hungry?"

"I lost Jerimiah. My retainer, my ghoul. I'm almost certain the mortals got him. I was so angry, and
so upset, and the court needs to know, and then I got here and I just *couldn't*." She collected
herself. "I'm sorry. I'm Adisa, of the Banu Haqim."

"I am Lady Catherine, and we do have an audience. We could tell them for you. Do you have somewhere
safe to go?"

"I have a haven, but I won't be staying there tonight. Tell the Prince the humans are hunting us."

"We had our own run-in a few nights ago. If you need it, we have room at our haven." Catherine wrote
down the address and gave it to her.

Adisa thanked her and left the party.

---

They found Chun Hei talking to two or three others. Alice could see what she was doing: vetting
people.

"Can I help you?"

"Perhaps we can help you," said Alice. "You're looking for someone you treasure?"

"I'm looking for the killers of my sire."

"I'm looking for a fight," said Catherine. "And we heard you might know where to find Gwenllian
ferch Arwyn."

"I like fights as much as the next man," said Matthias, "if the next man doesn't like fights. But
tell us who is missing."

"Zawadi was my sire." She talked for a while. Matthias noticed that she knew Ayse. And his years in
the law let him read something else under what she said. Chun Hei was a bastard of sorts, a childe
made without leave. Normally she would have been destroyed. Arwyn, it seemed, had found a way to
keep her.

They all came away sure of one thing: Chun Hei was convinced that humans had murdered Zawadi.

---

Lady Scarlett found them, with Tony beside her.

"The Prince of London will see you now."

They went into a private room, and the room went away.

---

They were stepping down from a carriage. The clothes were of the seventeenth century. It was night,
and they were following someone into a large, beautiful, quiet house.

Matthias knew the man in front of them. It was Valerius, the present Seneschal, who was not at the
party tonight. Anne's sire.

Inside, Anne sank into a deep curtsey. Her gown was magnificent. Across the room, in the shadows,
stood three figures.

"I am honoured by your offer of an Embrace, into a life beyond life," Anne said. "But I am a mere
mortal, and there is a dilemma I simply cannot resolve. I have three competing offers, and they too
have come here tonight."

Nafeesa al-Niazi, of the Brujah. Captain Jonas Cardoza, of the Lasombra. And Lady Elizabeth
Moorhampton.
