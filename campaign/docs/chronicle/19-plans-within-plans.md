---
title: Plans Within Plans
part: Sunday, 25 March 2012
---

When they got back to the haven there was a car waiting. Matthias went to it with his dead rats and his dog, then
came into the haven long enough to hand Tony a card, and went back out to the car and was driven off.

The card greeted Tony as *sibling*. It named a meat-packing plant where he could meet Chun Hei.

---

It was dark when they got there, but there were people about by the warehouse and lights on in the offices.
Catherine walked straight up to the man at the door and tapped him on the shoulder.

"I'm looking for Chun Hei. She never said a time, but it's urgent."

"Who's asking?"

"Her sibling," said Tony.

"And friends of her sibling," said Catherine. It took some effort, but she caught the man's eyes and held them.
*Take us to Chun Hei.* He called up to three others on a steel balcony.

Alice could see what they were: ghouls, and Brujah ones.

They came back down and said it was all right to bring them in, and took them to Chun Hei.

---

Oskar Anasov had come to her, Chun Hei said, and told her to get in touch with Alice. She had made the connection
from the Elysium, and reached out to Tony instead. She and Tony were in the same place, she said. Zawadi, her
sire, was dead. Saeth, Tony's sire, was dead too.

When they asked where Arwyn was, she hedged. She asked them to help get Arwyn out, and without a bloodbath. Arwyn
was surrounded by mortals on high alert. Chun Hei's own face was known, but she could send her retainer, Kim Sun,
to go with them. Arwyn was hiding in the British Museum. And Chun Hei made it clear that if Arwyn wanted to, she
could cut her own way out.

They said they would make a plan, and go to the museum the next night to look it over.

---

Back at the haven they thought about helping Regina with Trenton. Alice found a full plan of Bishopsgate police
station.

found out there was a gala the following night. He got them tickets.
