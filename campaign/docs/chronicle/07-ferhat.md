---
title: Ferhat
part: Wednesday, 21 March 2012
---

Tony rang the number Najma had left. Whoever answered said the guru was not available but would be
at the library. He asked what Najma looked like. Tony told him, and said she looked a bit anaemic.

"Ah yes," said the voice. "I know the type."

From the phone box Tony watched an older woman hold up a device, its screen filming a crowd of
youths. In the middle of it two of them were squaring up with knives. The crowd shuffled back and
forth around them until it had penned in the phone box.

Tony let the shadows take him. He edged back toward the street. One of the boys connected and cut
the other, who dropped his knife. A friend grabbed the cut arm, lifted it, and began lapping up the
blood. In the open, in front of everyone, and on the woman's little screen.

Tony was out of the box before anyone could see him move. He took the phone out of the woman's
hand, took the boy who was drinking by the other arm, and dragged him into an alley.

The boy fought. Tony got him in a headlock.

"What on earth were you thinking?!"

"I don't know, bruv, I can't help it! I was out with my mates last week and this dope fiend jumps
out from under the bridge and bites me. Do I need drugs? Is it methamphetamines?"

Tony twisted his arm up behind his back and had him lead the way to the bridge. On the way he said
his name was Ferhat. There was nobody under the bridge now.

They took a cab. Tony cowed the driver without a word, and they arrived at the safe house. Darius
was there.

Darius was sorry to hear Ferhat had been attacked, and asked if he could examine him. He looked in
Ferhat's mouth. The fangs were so small they could not have broken skin. He brought out some bagged
blood, and Ferhat drank it, and was full, and did not understand. Darius and Tony began to teach
him what he was.

Ferhat worked something out on his own. "Is that why Najma weirded me out?"
