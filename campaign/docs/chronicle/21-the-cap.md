---
title: The Cap
part: Monday, 26 March 2012
---

Ferhat, Matthias, Oliver, Kim, Catherine, Tony and Alice went round the Roman Britain galleries behind
Gwenllian.

Nothing seemed to touch her, though Matthias thought something was getting under her skin. Still, she seemed at
ease in all that crowd of mortals.

In the Roman gallery her stories settled into an ordinary guide's patter. Matthias was working out how he would
pull a robbery.

About forty feet from the cap, an elderly couple were reading the card beside it, and Catherine turned to
Gwenllian.

"Would you help us get that cap? It's herald business."

Gwenllian stopped short and turned to face her, stiffly. "I have served my god long and faithfully. I have seen him
lift his servants into the light, and I have seen him burn his enemies to ash. It is the sun's right to do as he
sees fit. As he did with you. Do you know what was done to you?"

"No."

"You carry my blood, and the blood of everyone who served him most closely. I don't know whether you kept a soul.
That was the price of the duty he asked of you."

"I will do my duty and bring him back," said Alice. "We need the cap to do it. He is among us."

Gwenllian drew herself up, and her voice cracked across the room. "Excuse me! This area of the museum needs to be
cleared." She sent people to the exits, grumbling and muttering, and then sent the security guard out after them.

---

It came to Alice all at once. She remembered the Blooding, and the times it had failed before her. The ritual had
been meant to wipe her out, and leave only the perfect herald, the perfect obedient servant. Somehow she had
clawed back pieces of herself. And the flashbacks were memories, she understood now. Memories from the blood of
everyone who had given it.

---

With the room empty, Gwenllian went to the case, let it scan her eye, unlocked it, and took out the cap. She put it
gently into Alice's hands.

Matthias could not believe that they had just asked for it and been given it.

"I think we're done here," said Alice.

"Come with us?" said Catherine. "You could see his return."

"Honestly, I've stayed here as long as I have to guard the thing you're holding. So yes." She looked at Kim, and
nodded. "I'll come with you. Has the Prince made a ruling yet?"

Kim shook her head.

"We haven't been able to reach Queen Anne since the Elysium," said Catherine.

"Why did you do what you did?" said Alice.

Gwenllian looked lost for a moment. "I think the accusation is true. I think I did kill him. I don't remember doing
it. I remember going to Bath to meet him. And I remember coming back and hearing he was dead."

---

Arwyn took them down through the lower floors and the service corridors, and they came out a few streets from the
museum. They argued about where to go. In the end she agreed to stay with Catherine and the rest. She sent Kim back
to Chun Hei.

They told her about Richard de Worde. Alice showed her the necklaces. Matthias told her about his maps and his rats.
Tony told her about the files. Gwenllian agreed to go into the Underground with them.

Alice had found a book with a ritual for bringing back memory. It looked possible.

Matthias wanted to lay his hands on one of Gwenllian's teeth. Catherine tried to sweet-talk her into it, and Arwyn
was *not* having it, and when Catherine leaned on her with her will, Arwyn's eyes flashed and she told Catherine to
step away. Catherine stepped away at once.

Alice tried to explain what Matthias meant to do.

"Of all people," said Gwenllian, "I would have expected a Tremere to understand that giving something of yourself to
another lays you open for good. I will give nothing of myself to anyone but my Lord."

All he needed to do, Matthias said, was touch it. There was a great deal of arguing. In the end Gwenllian pulled
the tooth. She held it out to Matthias, and turned to Alice.

"I had been Kindred for a thousand years before the Tremere killed Saulot."

Matthias touched the tooth, and it ran past him fast. Gwenllian killed Jackdaw. She did not drink his soul. She got
rid of the body, and then she went to Queen Anne and told her, and Queen Anne wiped it from her mind.

Matthias told them.

"The bitch!" said Alice.

Gwenllian's face was very still. "That will have been the last time."

---

The five of them put on the necklaces. With Matthias's rats and his dog behind them, they went down into the
Underground to face Richard de Worde.
