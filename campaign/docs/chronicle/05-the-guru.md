---
title: The Guru
part: Wednesday, 21 March 2012
---

Catherine went back to the safe house. Alice and Tony were not there. Darius was, reading
something, and he put it away when she came in.

She was hungry, and short-tempered with it, and she had come back suspicious of everything.
"Where are they?!"

"They didn't say."

"What are you reading? Why did you hide it?"

"A report," Darius said, "for Queen Anne. For Friday."

She took the BlackBerry out of his hand and demanded to know what it was. He spent some time
walking her through how a mobile worked. She could just about manage the basics. The rest of it
overwhelmed her. He told her she could buy a cheap one of her own at any of the little shops.

---

She went to the banker's flat. Nobody was home.

She looked around. There was a laptop. A filing cabinet opened for her: share certificates,
accounts in the Caymans, and more. She took what she could make sense of. The sums ran to
millions of pounds, which staggered her. There was nothing in it about her sire, and nothing about
the cult. Otherwise the flat was bare. She wrote down the account numbers.

When Oliver came home he was well drunk, and there was a young woman with him. They came through
the door kissing, fumbling with keys and clothes. The woman pushed him down onto the bed, saw
Catherine already in it, and shrieked.

Catherine sat up as if she had just woken. "Darling, you brought a guest."

Oliver stared at her with his mouth open. He was drunk, and she was stunning.

"I thought you lived alone!"

Catherine held out her hand. "I'm Cat."

The woman was doing well for herself, but she was not *rich*. It settled down, in the end, and
Catherine got them both into bed with her, and fed on them both. Then she turned to the woman.

"I'd like to give you a gift."

She bit her own tongue and kissed her, and let her have a little of her blood. It was the start of
making a ghoul. She did the same for Oliver.

"You are such a powerful man. You would like for me to be by your side."

She talked him into managing her business interests, starting with some of his own money, and
into introducing her to other people of power in finance. He began to get excited. He talked about
alternative energy in the North Sea, and about something called Bitcoin, which was still very new.

She told him she needed somewhere to stay, and what it needed to be for her and for some friends.
He promised to have somewhere by Friday.

---

Tony had stormed off after Alice went for Najma.

Alice found a phone box and rang the number on the flyer. It rang twice and was picked up.

"Psychic Healing."

"Hi, I received this number from Najma. May I speak with..."

"Ah. You wish to speak with the guru."

"Yes, if he's available tonight. My name is Alice Mockingdale."

A pause. "The guru has retired for the evening. He will be meeting at Southall Library tomorrow
evening, and new disciples are always welcome. We have an onboarding process for seekers, and
perhaps you can ask the guru your question."

"Thank you. I know him from *many*, *many* years ago. If you give him my name, he will understand
why I need to see him right *now*."

The voice went cold. "If you believe you know the guru from a prior life, I assure you he is quite a
different man." He hung up.

She rang again. It went to voicemail.

---

The library was shut. Alice found the trail anyway, the traces people leave behind them, and it was
a large group. She followed it out of the middle of Southall. The houses got nicer. They were near
Osterley now, among gates and gated closes, and the trail thinned as the group split up, until it
was too faint to follow and ended somewhere among two or three houses.

One of them stood out to her like the sun. It was covered in wards, and some of them were set
against followers of Mithras. She took that one down without trouble. That it was there at all
made her furious. Then she remembered that Mithras had thought very little of the Tremere as a
rule, with a few exceptions. She had been one. Sri Sansa had been another.

She climbed the gate. Her dress caught at the top and she went over the other side in a heap. A
security light came on and she scrambled into cover. Nobody came out.

At the side of the house there was a sliding glass door. Another motion light clicked over her
head, but its bulb was dead. She could not get the door open. In the end she was angry enough, and
hungry enough, to put her fist through the glass, and she went in.

The ground floor was for entertaining: a big open kitchen, several fridges full of catering platters
already made up, and no blood anywhere, which was strange. There was a large open room with cushions
on the floor to sit on. A wide staircase went up to a mezzanine, and a narrow spiral one went up to
the floor above.

---

Before dawn Catherine slipped out of the flat. She did not know where to find the others, but she
did remember where the Tremere Primogen lived, and she paid Edward Bainbridge a visit.

A ghoul opened the door. "Lady Catherine."

She asked to see his master. The ghoul said he would check, and shut the door in her face. He came
back after some time. "He will entertain your visit, even if unannounced."

The house was full of objects, masks and statues from Africa most of all. A room that had once been
used for rituals was full of books and diagrams now.

"Ah," Bainbridge said. "You have returned from burial, it would seem."

"I had to reacquaint myself."

"It must have been a shock to come back and find your god is dead."

"You can't truly kill a god."

"Well, then he's doing as good a job of absenteeism as the Creator. What brings you here?"

"I'm trying to understand the modern world, and would like you to help me with something."

"I'll listen. But I'm not sure what you have to offer in exchange."

"First, I am looking for a Lady Darkwood. Second, a particular artifact. You are a curator of
artifacts, are you not?"

"I have a very *specific* collection. If it falls within my interests, I almost certainly have it or
know where to find it. If it falls outside them, I am likely of no use to you."

"Mithras had a ruby ring."

"Well, I am not much for jewellery, but I know of the ring you mean. And you said Darkwood? Tonight
I'll waive payment, since the only information I have for you is unhelpful. The ring, I believe, is
with Sri Sansa. He is no longer welcome in the chantry. He is trying to split the clan further and I
will not tolerate it." He told her roughly where Sansa could be found. "Darkwood was among the buried,
during the bombings in '41."

Grief went through Catherine. She knew, deep down, that Angelique did not walk the night. And a
memory came to her without being called. Part of why she had agreed to be a herald was the hope
that when she woke, Angelique might have woken too.

"Where is her resting place?"

"That was decades ago, love. I wouldn't know where to begin."

"It was decades since we were buried."

"And look what a bloody hard time we had finding you. It was dumb luck got you out of the ground. Come
to the Elysium. There might be someone there who knows where the body was last seen."

---

Alice went up the stairs. At the top was a private study, half packed, as if someone were moving in
or out. There was a great deal of occult nonsense on the shelves, which disappointed her. A light was
on further down the hall.

She went through the papers. They were about moving people and things: property bought, houses for a
few dozen people, in rural England. Sri Sansa was getting out of London.

She went down the hall. Through a door that stood a little open she could see him, packing things
into book boxes.

Alice opened the door. "Are you going anywhere?"
