---
title: Safe and Unsafe Houses
part: Friday, 23 March 2012
---

The cab left Alice, Matthias and Jack at the old chantry. Alice took them in and they went back to
questioning Jack.

Through Catherine's eyes Matthias had seen Valerius kill Diane, and order Tony to drive, before he lost
the connection. Tony and Catherine had both looked all right.

Matthias made himself small and harmless-seeming, and Jack began to relax. When he learned that Diane
was dead he was badly shaken.

"What do you know about Queen Anne?" Matthias asked.

"She's been trying to put Valerius out to pasture for a while now. Valerius has been making plans in
case. We found out about a few stash houses, safe houses, that Anne kept secret from the court. If you
want to deal with Valerius for good, I'd start there." He gave them the addresses of two.

"You said Valerius did his best," said Alice. "What did you mean?"

"I mean he *believes* he killed Mithras. We *know* he didn't."

"How exactly did you meet Mithras? In a vision?"

"He came to us. In the flesh."

Alice looked at him hard for a moment, told him not to move, and took Matthias into the next room.

The face Matthias had sensed on the tablet, he told her, looked *nothing* like Mithras. But Jack was
sure of it. Since Jack seemed honest, Matthias thought they should look at the safe houses.

Alice wanted to taste Jack's blood again. Matthias went and got it for her. This time the memories came
flooding. Jack ***was*** blood-bound, to Mithras himself.

She was stunned, and told Matthias. He searched his memory. In diablerie, at least, it was possible for
the blood to take over the one who drank it. Perhaps the Banu Haqim or Ventrue he had seen *was*
Mithras, in a new body. With the Hecata, or the Tremere, he might learn more.

Matthias was hungry and started toward Jack. Alice stopped him. Now that they knew Jack was bound to
Mithras, they would find someone on the way.

On the way out Matthias fed on someone by the side of the road while Alice and Jack kept watch. Then they
took a cab to the first address.

---

Catherine's thoughts ran on while Valerius gave Tony directions one turn at a time, first to lose any
tail and then into the City. They pulled into the car park under a block of flats and walked round to a
door on a side street. Valerius keyed a code and took them up to a flat on the second floor, and keyed
another.

It was elegant and comfortable, and nobody had lived there in a long time. Tony dropped into a chair.
Valerius paced.

Catherine offered to fetch Oliver to help get Valerius out of London. Valerius thanked her and put it
off. Instead he took down a painting and keyed a third code, and a vault door opened.

It was like her new haven, but nothing about it had been rushed. It looked almost like a museum. There
was a beautiful Victorian dress on a mannequin, and jewellery, and old books and diaries and pamphlets
going back through the 1900s, the 1800s, the 1700s. The 1600s?

And there, under glass, was Mithras' seal.

"Valerius. Could I have this little trinket here?"

He was putting books into a banker's box. "Take whatever catches your eye. I have no use for it."

Catherine put the seal in her pocket. Then she found luggage in the bedroom and began packing: the dress,
some of the papers, then the jewellery.

One piece stopped her dead. It was a necklace from Angelique, the one she had worn at the ritual in the
forties.

She held it up at Valerius. "Where did you get this?"

He looked at her blankly. "This is Anne's collection, not mine."

"Really? That bitch stole it off my neck while I slept?"

Valerius went back to his packing.

Catherine fastened it gently round her own neck, and went on going through the place without a qualm.

---

Alice, Jack and Matthias got out at the first address and told the cabbie to wait. Matthias looked at the
keypad by the door for a moment, and touched it, and the code came to him. He keyed it in without a
pause. The door opened. He followed the same trace up the stairs to the flat on the second floor, keyed
that code as well, and they were in a nicely furnished flat.

They split up to search. Matthias went into the main bedroom and found two men: an anti-terrorism officer
and an intelligence agent. The wardrobe stood open, everything in it thrown onto the bed, and at the back
of it was a vault. The intelligence man sat on the floor with wires running from the vault to the laptop
in front of him.

The officer saw Matthias move and brought his rifle up. He fired as Matthias called up two tendrils of
shadow. The round was incendiary, and it burned Matthias badly through the Kevlar. The shadows took hold
of the two men.

Jack and Alice came running. Alice spat venom at the officer. He dodged it and it hissed against the wall.
Jack went for him, and the man drew a pistol and shot Jack through the collarbone, and Jack went down.

The shadows tightened, and the two men passed out. Matthias was hurt, but not badly enough to die of it.
He saw to Jack and stopped the bleeding, and then looked at the laptop. It was running a code-breaker on
the vault. It had another thirty or forty-five minutes to go.

"It's safe to come out now," Matthias called, in case anyone was hiding in the vault. Nobody answered.

Alice took the men's weapons, cuffed them and searched them. Each carried several sets of identification
and security passes. Both had GCHQ and Antigen. There was JTRIG, the Joint Threat Research Intelligence
Group, and SO13, and the man with the laptop had ECTU.

Matthias noticed that their weapons matched the ones Queen Anne had offered them. "Are these her people,
perhaps?"

Alice asked Jack what the letters meant. He could not tell her much.

They waited for the code-breaker. When the vault opened they went in. It was fitted out much like the one
where Catherine and Tony were.

Dawn was coming. They barricaded themselves inside.
