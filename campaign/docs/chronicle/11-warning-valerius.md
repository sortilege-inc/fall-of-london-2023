---
title: Warning Valerius
part: Friday, 23 March 2012
---

When Lady Scarlett had made the introductions, Catherine was taken aside into another room and
questioned by the Sheriff of North London, Letitia Shy.

"Why did you choose to feed in a domain you had no claim to?"

"Forgive me. When I woke I was given blood. I was under the impression that I could not speak to the
Prince until the Elysium. What was I supposed to do in the meantime?"

"You may always seek an audience. And was the safe house not stocked? Did Darius not provide for you,
or offer to let you hunt on his grounds?"

"Darius had *bagged* blood. It did not suit my taste."

Letitia Shy laughed. "And?"

"What is my punishment?"

"You will owe the Queen a boon. And until it's negotiated otherwise, please keep your feeding to this
domain." She handed over a map with a square marked on it.

"Does that mean my bank man is off limits?"

"Is he in this square?"

"I can bring him there."

"I will pretend I did not hear that. Please do not make the mistake of repeating yourself, or of
making me repeat myself."

When Catherine came out, only Lady Scarlett was waiting.

"You missed some of the discussion while you were occupied. Can I draw your attention to something?"
She opened a dossier and began turning the pages. "Your Prince has asked that you and your fellow
heralds do the honours. It should speak for itself. Do ask me if you have any questions."

It was all there: where Valerius was staying, the route he would take tonight, and the ways he might
be helped to his final death.

"So we're meant to kill this person?"

"The one who claims to have killed Mithras."

---

Catherine found Matthias, and he told her it was true.

"We said 'yes, your highness'. We don't necessarily mean to follow through. We think she's covering
her tracks. She let slip that *she* knows Mithras isn't dead, and that knocks the bottom out of her
reason for killing Valerius. I don't much trust her."

Catherine lowered her voice. "Why don't we turn it around and kill her instead?"

"I thought of that. There's just the wrinkle that she's the bloody queen. We need more to go on
first, and Valerius might give it to us."

"As prince, she can authorise a killing, can't she?"

"Technically. But this isn't a blood hunt, so it's a killing outside the law. And she could always say
we did it on our own account and call a blood hunt on *us*."

They went back into the party. They heard stories of Mithras coming and going. Plenty of people
thought he was dead, though maybe that was only a game everyone was playing. Maybe he had gone east,
answering the Beckoning. But if Mithras had been killed, Valerius was the one everyone suspected.

Catherine found Lady Scarlett again and apologised, and asked her to put a question to the Queen:
could someone dig under one of Queen Anne's buildings, for Angelique?

"I have no objection to work that can be done privately and without risk to the structure. I can put
you in touch with someone to oversee it. It would have to be done within the fortnight."

"Thank you." Catherine moved to embrace her. Lady Scarlett took her hand instead and pressed it lightly.

"I look forward to hearing of your success tonight."

---

Tony arrived late and found Catherine and Matthias with their heads together.

"Did Queen Anne have anything on the thing we're looking for?"

"Oh yes," said Matthias. "Had a lovely chat. She said she had the seal and would be happy to give it
to us. Wanted us to kill her sire. You know. The usual."

"Are we hitmen for hire, then?"

"I'm not."

"I don't think this is the place to be talking about it," Catherine said.

"So if we don't do it, we don't get the seal?"

"It won't vanish," said Matthias. "It just might be harder to come by without Anne's say-so."

"She's said it isn't *here*," said Alice.

Tony stopped someone to ask for an audience and was turned away, curtly. It went to his head.

"That's outrageous! You can't deny a herald!"

He started swinging. Two security ghouls got hold of him. He threw one off, twisted round and sank his
teeth into the other, who went slack and dazed, just as the first hit Tony square in the face.

"Tony, Tony, Tony," said Catherine. "They're not worth it. Not the place, not the time."

She could see they could leave now, or Tony would be thrown out. She smacked him on the nose.

"Bad Tony!"

She held him in check, barely, long enough for Matthias to get them all to the lift and down to the car
park. Tony told himself that holding back made him better than the ghouls.

---

In the car they argued. Back to the haven? Go after Caleb's lead and Richard de Worde? Find Adisa? Or do
what Anne had asked?

Catherine thought about buying time by killing the competition. Matthias could not tell who the trap was
for: Valerius, the heralds, or both. And what if her sire was in on it? They did not even know whether he
could be won over.

"I think we send one of us in to talk," said Matthias, "and the rest of us wait in ambush."

Catherine said she might be able to seduce him.

"You don't know he'll be buying what you're selling."

In the end Catherine went in, with Matthias to follow if she got eaten, or something like it.

---

The haven was in the Docklands. A car much like theirs was parked nearby. Catherine walked to the door
and pressed the buzzer. There was a camera.

"One moment."

A security guard opened the door. "Come in." He looked past her. "Are there more?"

"They're keeping an eye out."

He took her down the hall to a side room, where a woman guard joined them, and then into a sitting room.
Valerius sat there: long hair, looking about thirty, swivelling impatiently in his chair and holding a
book against his chest.

"This is a little different."

"I have come to pick you up."

"Sounds like it's all arranged with Jack and Diane here. Are you ready to go?"

"Are Jack and Diane going first?"

"Oh, they're my bodyguards. They're coming with me."

"Could I give you a message from her majesty first? Privately?"

"You may speak freely in front of them."

Catherine's patience went. "Shit goddamn motherfucker!" She looked around, went down on her knees, and
reached up to him. "Your life is in danger. Anne sent us here to kill you."

"And you have evidence of this?"

She reached into her bag. Jack kept his hand on his holster. She gave Valerius the dossier and he leafed
through it.

"That conniving bitch," he murmured. He sounded impressed.

"The others in the car," said Jack. "They know you're telling him this?"

"They know."

"So they're no threat to Valerius."

"They are not."

"Then I recommend we keep to the plan we have," said Jack, "now we all agree on getting Valerius safely
out of London and out of Anne's hands."

"I would like to ask you something," said Catherine, "as a herald of Mithras."

"I *knew* I recognised you!"

"You have been accused of killing him."

"And yet you warn me, to save my life."

"I don't pass judgement without proof. I want proof that you're the one who killed him. Either way, I'll
save you tonight."

"I don't know what proof of me you'd want," said Valerius. "There is nothing left of Mithras. I took his
lover. I had found out who the lover was, and I knew that bait would bring him. I drew him to the
Gunpowder Mills, and once he was inside we blew up the whole building. I watched and waited for months to
be sure he was gone. Then I told Anne. Do you still want to pardon me?"

"Why did you do it?"

"You're a Ventrue. You know what power can do. Mithras did a phenomenal amount for London, but he had no
vision for *England*. None for *Europe*, or the world. The Camarilla did. I brought them here, and I was
punished for it. I brought the idea of Kindred united across the kingdom and across Europe, where our
power could actually be used, and Mithras had eyes only for London. He could not see past it. When the
reprisals came, and the demotion, I bit my tongue and bided my time."

Catherine leaned in and whispered. "The only real reason I agreed to be one of his heralds was so that I
could wake and be with my sire again." She sat back. "I'll pretend I didn't hear your confession. I'll
still save you. Come on."

Valerius nodded to Jack and Diane. They picked up the last of the bags and they went.

Catherine did not know that Matthias had been riding her senses the whole time. He did not tell her, and
he did not tell the others.

---

Jack offered to drive one car, and Matthias and Alice went with him. Valerius and Diane took the other,
with Tony and Catherine.

As they pulled away Jack turned to Alice and Matthias. "Are you heralds of Mithras too?"

Alice said nothing. Matthias laughed.

"Do I look like a bloody herald?"

"Sorry," said Jack. "My mistake."
