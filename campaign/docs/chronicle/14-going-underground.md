---
title: Going Underground
part: Saturday, 24 March 2012
---

Alice and Matthias woke on Saturday night. Jack had sat up over the two men all day, cuffed and gagged.
After some talk, Alice went to find the station Caleb had named, where Richard de Worde could be found,
and Matthias said he would meet her there.

From the vault Alice took a Mithraic cult robe and three of Valerius' journals.

---

Before the day, Catherine had rung Oliver and had him charter a private flight for Valerius, and told him
to arrange for it to blow up in the air. She did it on her own. As far as she was concerned, Valerius had
served his purpose.

She woke to a missed call from him and rang back.

"Everything is in place. Come and see me for the details."

She told Tony she was going out.

---

Tony used the time to ask Valerius about de Worde, and about Arwyn.

Arwyn's name shocked him. "She is on the Red List." Wanted, to be destroyed on sight, with a reward on
her, for the rumoured diablerie of Prince Jackdaw of Bath. "And Arwyn had been spreading stories that
Mithras had joined the Sabbat, which didn't win her many friends either."

Valerius could want a man dead and still want his name kept clean. He had feared Mithras and respected him
even while he fought him. He thought Mithras was *wrong*. He never thought him dishonourable, or evil.

"Mithras' weakness was his lover, Roger de Camden," he said. "But nobody knew what de Camden looked like.
My plan began when de Worde came to me, unasked, and told me that de Camden was in fact living disguised as
Pater Thomas. I used that to set the lure for the attack that killed Mithras."

De Worde had shut himself away underground, and used magic to keep other Kindred from coming too close.

Tony remembered meeting Chun Hei, the childe of Zawadi, whom Chun Hei believed hunters had killed. And
Zawadi had been Arwyn's childe.

---

Oliver showed Catherine the itinerary: a charter leaving from an airfield in north-west London. He had it on
good authority that it would explode over water on the way to Scotland. Catherine congratulated him and gave
him more of her blood.

"Anything you require," he said. "I will move mountains for it."

Claire came in while she was there. "Did you see the news about the hostage situation?"

"Enlighten me."

"They said terrorists took over part of the Shard. They evacuated the rest."

Catherine was shocked. "Did they name any casualties?"

"They haven't released anything about casualties or hostages. But the news won't shut up about it, and
there was this annoying cordon downtown I had to get through to come here. It was a pain in the arse."

Catherine kissed Claire and let her taste her blood too. Claire's shoulders came down and the tension went
out of her.

Catherine had Oliver drive her back to the safe house, making sure they were not followed.

---

She told Valerius the itinerary. He picked up two duffel bags, mostly books and papers.

"I have what I need. I'll have your driver take me straight to the airfield."

"There's something else. A mortal told me about a terrorist attack on the Shard, where the Elysium was held
last night."

"It isn't impossible. But Queen Anne has plenty of enemies who aren't mortal, and this looks like a useful
pretext. It's best I leave the city sooner rather than later. Though it would be delicious if her attempts on
me ended up bringing her down."

When he had gone, Catherine whispered to Tony. "We have what we came for. Tonight that motherfucker's going
to fry."

Tony was stunned. He said nothing.

---

Back at the flat they found Adisa, the Banu Haqim from the Elysium. Ferhat was talking to her, and Claire was
seeing to things and looking puzzled.

"Oh, thank goodness," said Adisa. "You heard about the attacks?"

"Yes," said Catherine. "Do you know anything about them?"

"I left before you did. I hoped you'd know. I hope it's all right that I took you up on your offer. I didn't
feel safe in my haven after everything."

"We were sent on an errand by Queen Anne," said Tony. "We've only just finished."

"We should probably check in with her," said Catherine. "I'm not sure how."

---

Alice went down the stairs to the station without a sound, past the police officers and their dog who were
keeping an idle eye on the place.

The station was disused. There was someone else in the dark down there, a young man in a button-up shirt and
khakis, wandering with no purpose she could see. At one point he caught a rat and drank from it.

She went on, found the station Caleb had meant, and felt the way on through the tunnels. It was heavy with
magic.

The back of her neck prickled. She froze. She was being watched. She held herself completely still and stared
down the tunnel, and part of the shadow turned over and climbed up the wall and crawled away, deeper in. There
was a low growl.

Alice backed off until she could see the young man again. Then she steeled herself and went back down.

---

Claire, Adisa, Tony and Catherine came after her. Claire went up to the police and kept them busy while the
other three slipped down the stairs.

The young man came to meet them. "Oh, hi. Are the police gone? My name's Victor."

"No," said Catherine. "The police are still here."

His face fell. "Oh. How did you get through, then? Are you looking for the club too?"

"We're supposed to be meeting someone."

"Same here!" He unfolded a piece of paper and showed them. It was a flyer for a Lonely Persons' Club. "I've been
here three days. Nobody else has come through, and I can't find the door, and I can't get past the police."

"Well, a friend of mine was supposed to be here." Catherine described Alice.

"I haven't seen anybody. But if you're down here long enough it feels like you're being watched."

They went on, and caught up with Alice.

Catherine told her about the attack on the Shard. Alice told them about the humans she and Matthias had found,
and the two things fit together.
