---
title: Jesus Take the Wheel
part: Friday, 23 March 2012
---

Matthias was still riding Catherine's senses.

Catherine raised the partition, so that Diane and Valerius had their privacy, and so did she and
Tony.

"All right, Tony. I do plan on having him killed. Not tonight, and not any time soon. He's worth more
to us alive than dead just now, and he has power we couldn't get at otherwise. We'll take him to our
safe house, and I'll have my banker arrange a flight, and while we wait maybe we can get more out of
him. But we can't take him to the warehouse, in case the other assassins are there."

She lowered the partition. "Seneschal, here's what we're going to do. There was no pick-up car.
We're going to take you to one of my places. I have a contact who can arrange safe passage. I just
need you to trust that we have your best interests at heart."

Valerius looked troubled. He spoke to Diane quietly, and then said, "I appreciate your candour. The
address?"

"How about your man follows us?" said Tony.

"Jack is perfectly capable of following you. And if we're followed, they can shake the tail and meet
us after."

"That's... perfectly reasonable."

Catherine gave the address. Diane called Jack on her radio, gave a code phrase, and then: "Change of
destination. We're going to lead, you're going to follow." She read out the address.

In the other car Matthias watched Jack type it into some kind of device and let Tony pull ahead.
Alice knew the address. It was Catherine's new haven.

---

"Is there anything we could show Queen Anne," Tony asked, "to make her think you're finished?"

"If you try to fool Anne, it will only hold so long, and then you'll need a way out as well," said
Valerius. "Your best defence is a good offence. That said, I know some of her safe houses, and you
might find something worth having in them. But if you're starting a coup, you're on your own."

"Valerius," said Catherine. "Why are you trusting me?"

"Trust isn't all or nothing. I don't trust you. I distrust you less than a great many at this court.
Those I do trust have seen fit to trust you. And frankly, that the Queen gave you this task at all is
a compliment in itself, even if you choose not to deserve it. Her judgement is rarely this far out."

Tony wondered whether he had recognised them as heralds. He said nothing.

"You're an elder," said Catherine. "You have centuries behind you. Why do you think Queen Anne wanted
you dead?"

Valerius smirked. "Why does any Ventrue do anything? What does my death get her?"

"Power. But she's already in the most powerful position there is."

"And when you're *in* power, you want to *stay* in power. And where is the greatest danger to your
power? Those who put you there."

"Why aren't *you* in power, instead of her?" said Tony.

"I have the flaw of vision. A vision larger than myself, large enough that I could see someone else was
better suited to carry it out."

"Do you think Anne still deserves her place?"

"Honestly? I'd have done the same, in her shoes. She'll deny this, or worse, if you repeat it to anyone.
But she has committed diablerie. In many ways she is more than my equal now."

"That's why she wants you dead," said Catherine. "You know her secret."

---

Diane's radio crackled. "Roadblock coming up," said Jack. "I know another way. Let me lead."

Matthias could see no roadblock ahead, and none on the screen Jack was looking at.

Jack's car sped up and pulled in front of Tony's. He turned to Alice and Matthias and said, with
meaning, "Sometimes the bull must be slain."

Matthias knew it for a Mithraic saying. He could not see what it had to do with anything. Alice missed
it entirely.

Jack signalled for an exit. Matthias sent arms of shadow round Jack's throat and grabbed for the wheel.
Jack twisted clear and wrenched the wheel over, and the car flipped.

Tony saw the car ahead turn over. He only just got round it, scraping the whole right side, and pulled
up twenty yards on.

"What the fuck is going on?" said Catherine. "Diane, what the hell?"

She turned. Diane had a stake and was trying to get it into Valerius. He tore through his seatbelt and
came up and sideways, fast. His eyes went cold, and he ripped her throat out.

Tony had one thought. They were on Queen Anne's payroll, and now she knew where the safe house was, and
that they had betrayed her.

---

Jack's car had come to rest on its roof. It was not burning. The shadow arms still had Jack. Alice
pulled herself out and spat paralysing poison over him.

Matthias got out too and went straight past her. He thought about the shotgun, remembered what it was
loaded with, and used his fists. He hurt Jack badly, and he meant to kill him.

Alice tried to stop him and could not. In the end she made it an order. "As your Heliodramus I command
you to cease! Let me question him first."

"Two minutes," said Matthias. "Then we need to be gone."

---

In the other car, Tony remembered that they had been asked to drive Valerius, and was given an address,
and drove off. Catherine felt the same will pressing on her and threw it off. *You're trying to dominate
another Ventrue?* She let it ride, to see where they were being taken.

Alice and Matthias watched the car pull away.

"I can find our friends wherever they are," Matthias told her.

"All right. Let me ask him a couple of questions, and then we go to the old chantry."

She made sure Jack was awake.

"Anne sent you, didn't she?"

"Why would I work with that usurper?"

"Why did you try to kill us?"

"Have you forgotten your lord so completely? I serve the same."

It sank in slowly. Jack was an acolyte of Mithras.

Matthias let the shadows go.

"You seek his return too?" said Jack.

"You know who I am," said Alice.

"Then you know why Valerius has to die."

"Are the rumours true? Did Valerius kill him?"

"He did his best."

"Do you have orders from our Lord," said Matthias, "or were you running off on your own doing stupid
things?"

"It is an edict from his own lips."

Alice could see that Jack believed every word.

"Matthias, we can't kill him yet. We'll take him with us."

---

Valerius brought Catherine and Tony to a safe house.

Alice, Jack and Matthias took a cab to the old chantry. On the way Alice tasted Jack's blood, and it went
badly.

Matthias laid his hands on the tablet from the car. He got a hazy sense of whoever had held it before
Jack: one of the Banu Haqim, maybe, or a Ventrue. Middle-aged. He could not make out the face. Perhaps,
he thought, some Ventrue had muddled Jack's mind and made him believe he was Mithras.
