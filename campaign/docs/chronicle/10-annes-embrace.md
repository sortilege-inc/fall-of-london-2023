---
title: Anne's Embrace
part: Friday, 23 March 2012
---

Even mortal, Anne already had the poise and the confidence.

Alice remembered who Valerius had been. Mithras' second-in-command, until he fell from favour for
letting the Camarilla set itself up in England.

Alice suggested tossing a coin between a test of arms and a test of wits. Anne said she could have
done that herself, if it were only a matter of chance.

They went through to the library. Anne took books off the shelves and put questions to them:
mathematics, science, the occult, medicine. Nafeesa answered with ease. Cardoza did well enough on
history and religion and struggled with the rest.

Watching Lady Moorhampton, they saw she was not what she appeared to be.

A question came on English common law. Matthias leaned in and whispered to Valerius where it came
from, and Valerius answered as if he had always known. On medicine, the answers Matthias helped with
would not make sense to anyone for another century.

It came down to Nafeesa and Valerius, and Anne set them to debate. They could see how much she enjoyed
it: beings far older and stronger than she was, jumping when she said jump.

Alice saw it then. Moorhampton was Malkavian.

Valerius finished the debate and left Nafeesa with nothing to say. Anne dismissed the others and took
Valerius away to be Embraced.

---

Back in the present, Matthias and Alice were a little shaken. Lady Catherine was not with them any
more.

Lady Scarlett presented them: heralds, who had come with Lady Catherine, who had brought a gift on
behalf of them all and asked for an audience.

"Well," said Anne, "you have my attention. I suggest you not waste it."

"We're hoping to reintroduce ourselves," said Matthias. "To understand how we can best serve."

Anne looked at them for a full minute without a word. Then she snapped her fingers.

"I know where I've seen you before. Everyone? Please excuse us."

The room emptied, all but Lady Scarlett.

"So. Mithras brought you forth. Is he back? Does he have need of something?"

"He'll be back when he needs to be," said Alice. "But we are making preparations for his arrival."

"He keeps to his own schedule," said Matthias.

"I'm all too familiar with that. Well, this is fortuitous. I have a task for you. You may have heard
the rumours of Mithras' death. In fact I had to declare him dead, to secure my authority in London.
How did I know?"

"How did you?"

"Because the one who claimed to have killed Mithras is my sire, Valerius. And I have laid a trap for
him. This very night, he believes he will be leaving London."

"And where is he going?"

"To the final death. And I cannot imagine a more fitting end than to have the heralds of the newly
returning Mithras strike the blow. I would give you a great boon for it."

"Why the sudden need?" said Alice. "He has looked after you for many years."

Anne laughed. "Death at my hand, even through a proxy, will be a mercy next to what Mithras would do
to him."

"How do you know it was him?"

"He confessed it to me straight away."

"Aren't you concerned that Mithras will replace you?" Matthias asked.

"I've always stepped aside when he's returned in the past."

They could tell she was not lying.

Lady Scarlett brought out a dossier: where he was, the route, the rest of it. Matthias thought it
had the smell of a set-up.

Alice remembered something. She was looking up out of a pool of blood. Anne stood over her, bleeding
into it, and behind Anne stood Mithras. It was the 1940s.

"What will you need?" Anne asked. "I'm happy to supply you."

"The seal, for one," said Alice.

"The seal? Oh, yes. I don't have it here, but I'm happy to give it to you when you get back."

"We'll need it first."

"I *already* have a plan in place, and it does not require the seal. I meant transport, firearms,
anything of that kind. And take this number. It is to be used only if every other avenue is
exhausted."

---

Vessels were brought in. Matthias drank one of them dry.

They took the lift behind the throne down to the sub-basement, and Lady Scarlett gave them a key card
so they could come back the same way. Matthias took a pump-action shotgun loaded with dragon's-breath
rounds. Alice took four Kevlar vests. They loaded everything into the car.

Alice and Matthias talked it over between themselves. They might only put Valerius into torpor, and
let Anne believe he was dead. Alice was worried about how hard it is to kill an elder. It was a
death trap.

Before they killed Valerius, she thought, they should fetch Tony and Catherine, and talk to him.
