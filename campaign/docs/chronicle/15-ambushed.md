---
title: Ambushed
part: Saturday, 24 March 2012
---

Matthias was late. At the station he saw a woman, Claire, with two officers, and a police van pulling away.
He found a broom and a bucket and tried to go down the stairs as a cleaner.

The woman officer tapped him on the shoulder. "The station's closed. It's off limits."

Matthias put on an Italian accent and pretended not to understand, and to be anxious about getting to work on
time. He pushed on. She caught his arm. The other officer, a big man, came forward, but the dog on his lead
was frightened of Matthias and would not go near him.

It was not going to work. Matthias dropped the broom and the bucket and ran down the stairs.

At the bottom there was nobody. He stepped into the shadows, and someone came out of them. It was Victor.
Matthias could see what he was. Victor told him that several heavily armed people had gone into the tunnels
just minutes before. Matthias thanked him and went after them.

---

Deeper in, Alice, Adisa, Catherine and Tony were taken by a terrible hunger. Alice recognised it for blood
sorcery, perhaps built into the tunnels themselves.

They talked about waiting for Matthias. Tony decided to run back and look for him.

He went back fast. On the way he passed a squad of armed police, wearing goggles and carrying no lights, and
startled them. He thought he could draw them off into a side chamber, and came at them with all the presence
he had. They were having none of it. Several of them fired. Tony was badly hurt.

---

Everyone in the tunnels heard the shots. Adisa charged. Alice and Catherine hurried after her, keeping low.
Matthias raised his arms of shadow and closed in.

He saw them before they saw him. Four humans in tactical gear. A fifth who carried a sword instead of a gun.
And a sixth, unarmed, who looked to be a thin-blood.

Tony slipped into the shadows and hoped very hard that his coterie would come through.

Matthias split the tendrils five ways and tried to throw the five humans at the vampire. The ones in tactical
gear were only distracted. The man with the sword was lifted and thrown, and fell on the thin-blood, and both
went down. Then Matthias lost himself. He wanted to hurt them and destroy them, all of them. The thin-blood had
put five fucking *humans* between herself and them.

Adisa went in and jumped on the back of one of the riflemen. She knocked the rifle out of his hands and tore at
his armour while he reached for his pistol.

Alice was seized by a need to do it properly, and to do it herself. She spat venom at the thin-blood, who saw
it just in time and dropped flat. She noticed Tony in the shadows.

Tony came out of them, grabbed a rifleman, and pulled him back in. He fed from him and held him up as a shield
at the same time, and he did not stop. He drained him dry.

Matthias split his shadow in two. One arm took the thin-blood and hoisted her in front of Alice's face. The
other reached for the one who seemed to be in charge, but the officer held something out and said words
Matthias did not know, and the arm came apart into nothing.

Adisa wrestled the pistol away from her man, and the swordsman ran her through the back.

"Drop it!" Catherine commanded. The man dropped the sword.

Alice spat again, straight into the thin-blood's face. It bubbled and boiled, and the thin-blood screamed.

Tony was sick with the feeding and the guilt of it, and he could have sworn there was a huge shadow standing
behind Alice. He used the man he had just killed to try and frighten the rest.

"I will rip your throat out too, if you don't freaking stop!"

The officer brought his rifle up and called to his men. "They're making threats! We have them on the back foot!"
He put three rounds into Tony's chest. One more would put him down.

---

Matthias kept his hold on the thin-blood. Adisa had her man disarmed and on the ground.

Something went over Alice in a rush of air. It was a monster, a beast of a thing, and not a werewolf. It took
the thin-blood by the leg and bent it until the knee snapped the wrong way. With its other hand it took one of
the riflemen by the head and tore the head straight off.

Catherine tried to hold the swordsman with her eyes. He shook it off.

Alice spat over Catherine's shoulder and caught him in the eyes. He screamed and went down with his hands over
his face.

Tony fell back again and let the blood knit him a little.

Matthias pulled at the thin-blood, and that angered the beast, which pulled back, hard. Matthias kept a leg. The
rest of the thin-blood went flying across the tunnel. Matthias reached for the officer again, and again the arm
dissolved.

"Tell them to stop!" Alice called.

Catherine drew herself up and gave the order to the officer, and to all of them. "Lower your arms. *Now*!"

The officer did. The beast took its chance. It took him by the chest in one hand and the legs in the other and
twisted him apart. Then it sprang away, back down the tunnel.

---

Adisa tied up the man she had brought down, and Catherine went to Tony. Matthias fed on the man with the burning
eyes.

Then Alice and Matthias went over to the thin-blood.

"Who are you?" said Alice. "What is your purpose?"

The thin-blood was in agony. "You fucking monster. Just put me out of my misery."

Alice asked again, and the thin-blood went quiet.

Matthias tried. "Tell us why you were sent to kill us, and I'll give you a quick death. Otherwise it will be
slow."

"An end is all I ask. Quicker would be better. I was assigned to this unit as part of the, you know, SO13 active
unit, under Paul Richards. He's the one that *thing* tore apart."

"You work for the humans?"

"I'm not keen on the disease I carry."

"What's your name?" said Alice.

"Gabrielle."

"What is SO13?" said Matthias.

"The Metropolitan anti-terrorism unit. I'm with the ECTU. Extraordinary Counter-Terrorism, you know?"

"Oh," said Matthias. "They *were* cops. Oh, shit."

He meant to tear her apart the moment they were done asking questions.
