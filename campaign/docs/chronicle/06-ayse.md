---
title: Ayse
part: Wednesday, 21 March 2012
---

Outside the toilets of the Blue Monsoon Lounge, Matthias took a good look at the young woman who
had asked him about his feed, and made her for Tremere.

"No such thing as a bad feed." He was ready to run.

"Are you here for the guru?" She leaned on him with her will, and it did not take, and she did not
notice. "Speak truly. Why do you seek Sri Sansa, *now*?"

Matthias went along with it. "I was recently reawakened. I'm seeking out old allies. I knew Sri
Sansa many years ago."

They found a booth. Her name was Ayse, she said, a Tremere of House Carna, and she made sure he heard
the house. Did he have views on the realignment of the Pyramid? He had no idea what she meant. She
went from feminism to Islam to the Camarilla to "ever since Vienna" and back, and in and out of it
all was what she wanted: Sri Sansa's support for Carna's claims.

Matthias let slip that he had been gone seventy years. Ayse put it together. She was awestruck. She
took his hands, and said it would be an honour if he would escort her to the meeting at Ealing
Library on Thursday night.

He asked about somewhere to stay nearby. She had rooms, she said, at the Meher Baba Centre. On the
walk there she told him how Meher Baba's understanding of Islam had helped her master the Beast,
for a while.

The rooms were downstairs, and secure, and plain, and they would do. Matthias did not trust her. He
could see that she wanted him close and happy very badly, for reasons of her own, whatever those
were.

Alone, with the door shut, he started rigging the entrance. Partway through, his thoughts turned to
death and would not turn back.
