---
title: The Gala
part: Sunday, 25 March 2012
---

Catherine went to Bishopsgate by the front door. It was four in the morning, and there were still half a dozen
people sitting about the lobby, one officer at the front desk and another behind.

She went up to the desk. "Darling."

"Do you have a case number?"

"I'm looking for Trenton? Trenton Adebayo?"

The officer typed the name and searched. "And your business with him is?"

"I'm his lover."

"Your business with him *here*, I mean."

"Well, his partner asked me to get him out of here. It's something of an open relationship."

She was using every bit of charm she had, and nothing more than that. Not in there.

More typing. "Bail is fifty pounds. Please sign this form."

At last Trenton came out.

"Trenton, darling! Regina wants to see you again."

His face went from confused to reassured, and they walked out together. He had been brought in drunk and
disorderly, he said, and it had been bumped up to assaulting an officer. He was visibly shaking. He had gone too
long without Regina's blood.

---

They went back to The Trunk. Regina was overjoyed.

Catherine told her they were trying to find Arwyn, and asked if Regina could get a message to her.

"This wouldn't have anything to do with the Red List, would it?"

Catherine assured her that it did not. The message was this: *The heralds of Mithras seek the property that is
theirs. Will you comply? And will you help with de Worde?*

Regina asked for two nights. By then she would either have managed it, and the boon would be settled, or she
would tell them where things stood.

---

Matthias sent his rats through the Underground and filled out his map: connections and service corridors that
were not on the plans, and a couple of places Rea seemed to go often.

---

On Monday, 26 March, Kim Sun came to the haven to go to the gala with them. A van picked them up: Alice, Tony,
Ferhat, Kim, Matthias, Catherine and Oliver.

The museum was busy, and they split up to look around. Matthias rode Catherine's senses again. There were
socialites, academics, young professionals, all sorts.

Oliver and Catherine went to the Roman galleries. Catherine was looking for Arwyn. She did not see her. She did
notice a tall Sikh man who looked like an officer, and then she made out some of the insignia on his chest:
Metropolitan Anti-Terrorism, and ECTU. He was watchful, but he did not seem to be on duty. He was deep in
conversation.

Matthias came into the gallery too and looked at the busts, and then walked straight into it: an exhibit that
was, beyond any doubt, the Phrygian cap they were looking for. He marked it for later. Someone brushed against
him. He turned, and there was a tour group, and at the head of it was Gwenllian ferch Arwyn.

---

All at once they were in the past. It was Roman London. The Ventrue Marcus Verus was questioning his childe about
the building of the mithraeum, the house of the worship of Mithras.

The scene changed. Verus stood before a god of light, blazing like the sun. It was Mithras himself.

"Who do you love more than me?"

Verus said there was no one. Mithras did not accept it, and asked again.

Verus gave way. His childe, Arcadius, held a deeper place in his heart.

Mithras accepted the honesty of it, and handed Verus the cap. "I am entrusting this to you, my beloved servant.
If it is ever a question of saving yourself or this cap, you must choose the cap."

Verus took it and bowed low.

A third vision. Verus and Mithras stood together, and they were looking through Arwyn's eyes. Mithras was sending
Verus abroad, and had chosen Arwyn to guard the cap in his place.

Matthias felt a wave of dread. He wanted to be rid of the thing before he had even touched it. Catherine wondered
whether the cap had anything to do with making peace with the Beast.

"Lord Verus serves me well," said Mithras, "but I need him elsewhere. I have chosen you, Arwyn, of all my
faithful, to take his holy charge from him. I know you may be unwilling. It may look to you like the badge of
your conqueror. Take it instead as the badge of your god."

Arwyn raged at being subjugated twice over, once as a Briton and again when she lost her mortality. But she was
devout, and she accepted.

---

Their senses came back to the present.

Catherine's eyes went round the room and landed on Arwyn across it. She took Oliver's arm and walked straight
over. Matthias hung back and sat down.

"How often are these tours given?"

Arwyn smiled. "This one's nearly over. I believe you're in my next group. We start at the Weston Gallery, Roman
Britain, in twenty minutes."

Catherine gathered the others and they went to the gallery. Matthias moved his borrowed senses to a guard in the
room with the cap.

Gwenllian came over, calm and sure of herself. "I believe you're my next tour group." She looked at Tony and took
both his hands. "It's been so long. It's good to see you."

Tony was a little thrown. He had not expected to be recognised, let alone warmly.

Arwyn turned to Kim Sun. "We can catch up after the tour."

Then she set off through the galleries and they followed. She was passionate and she knew everything, and it
began to dawn on them that she was as old as some of the thousand-year-old things in the cases.
